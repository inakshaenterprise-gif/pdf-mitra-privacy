PDF Mitra Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Welcome to PDF Mitra. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Mitra collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register or sign in, we may collect information such as your name, email address and account identifier, depending on the authentication method you use.

Analytics Information: We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve our services. This may include app interactions, device information, app version and identifiers.

Crash Information: We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports, diagnostic details, device information and technical information to identify and fix application problems.

Advertising Information: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may collect advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, ad interactions and related information for advertising and measurement.

2. PDF Files and Documents

PDF Mitra does not upload PDF documents to our own servers. PDF files are processed locally on your device for features that support local processing.

We do not use the contents of your PDF documents for advertising or analytics purposes.

If you choose to share a document through another application or service, that service's privacy policy will apply.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide application functionality.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.
  • To understand app usage through analytics.
  • To identify and fix crashes and technical issues.
  • To display and measure advertisements.
  • To maintain application security.
